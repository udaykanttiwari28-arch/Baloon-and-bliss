1. Home

Familiar, flexible and cost-conscious for intimate gatherings. Check space, parking and cleanup.

2. Condo Function Room

Often air-conditioned with room for catering, backdrops and photographs.

3. Restaurant

Combines food, seating and service with less cleanup. Confirm decoration rules.

4. Hotel Ballroom

Suitable for large guest lists, stages and wider installations.

5. Indoor Play Centre

Built-in activities for older siblings and young guests in a weather-independent setting.

6. Community Club

Practical function space; amenities and policies vary by location.

7. Rooftop Room

Modern surroundings and natural light. Prepare an indoor weather backup.

8. Garden Venue

Green surroundings with considerations for shade, humidity, wind and rain.

How to choose the right venue

Guest count

Allow room for seating, decorations, photography and safe movement.

Accessibility

Check lift access, parking, stroller routes, wheelchairs and elderly guests.

Air-conditioning

Indoor venues improve comfort and provide a stable environment for balloons.

Photography

Look for useful lighting and a clear area for the main backdrop.

Decoration space

Confirm width, ceiling height, loading access, setup times and restrictions.

Questions to ask

  • When may decorators begin?
  • Are balloons and backdrops permitted?
  • Is there loading and lift access?
  • What attachment restrictions apply?
  • What are cleanup deadlines?
  • What parking is available?
  • What is the outdoor weather plan?

Common venue mistakes

  • Waiting until preferred weekends are unavailable
  • Choosing a room too small for guests and decorations
  • Ignoring setup and decoration policies
  • Overlooking seating, access, air-conditioning or restrooms
