# What Affects First Birthday Decoration Pricing?

## 1. Venue

HDB homes, condo function rooms, restaurants, hotels, event halls and private venues have different access, space and setup requirements. Larger venues may need wider backdrops or additional decoration areas.

## 2. Balloon Styling

Options include organic balloon garlands, arches, columns, walls, ceiling balloons, entrance decorations and cake-table styling. More materials and installation time increase the price.

## 3. Personalised Backdrops

Backdrops can include the baby's name and age, custom colours, theme artwork, welcome signage or family name. Custom artwork and fabrication affect the quote.

## 4. Birthday Theme

Themes such as Teddy Bear, Jungle Safari, Dinosaur, Frozen Inspired, Butterfly Garden, Car Racing, building blocks, Princess, Unicorn and Baby Boss may require different props or artwork.

## 5. Decoration Size

### Small Setup

Suitable for homes and small gatherings. It may include a balloon garland, welcome sign or cake plinth.

### Medium Setup

Suitable for condo function rooms and restaurants. It may include a larger balloon installation, personalised backdrop, cake-table styling and props.

### Premium Setup

Suitable for hotels, ballrooms and larger events. It may include multiple installations, entrance styling, a photo area, stage backdrop, cake display and custom props.

# How to Keep Decoration Costs Manageable

## Focus on One Main Photo Area

A single statement backdrop gives guests a clear place for cake cutting and photographs without decorating the entire venue.

## Choose an Existing Theme Direction

Adapting a previous design can require less custom artwork than creating every element from scratch.

## Keep the Colour Palette Simple

Two main colours and one accent often look polished without unnecessary complexity.

## Confirm Venue Rules Early

Check access times, balloon restrictions, hanging rules and removal deadlines before finalising the design.

# Common Planning Mistakes

- Booking after preferred dates are already taken
- Ignoring venue access or decoration restrictions
- Choosing a setup that overwhelms the available space
- Forgetting how backdrop colours will look with family outfits and the cake
