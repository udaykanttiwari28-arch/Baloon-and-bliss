Choosing the right decoration

Consider venue size, ceiling height, guest count, theme, photography area and colour palette. The best setup complements the room instead of overwhelming it.

Current styling directions

  • Organic arrangements and mixed sizes
  • Matte and neutral palettes
  • Personalised signs and layered backdrops
  • Custom acrylic names
  • Elegant minimalist styling

Frequently asked questions

How long do balloon decorations last?

Indoor decorations should remain suitable throughout the event. Their condition afterward depends on balloon type, temperature and environment.

Can colours be customised?

Yes. Colours can match your theme, venue or preferred palette.

Are balloons suitable for homes?

Yes. Compact garlands and backdrops work well in HDB homes and function rooms.

Can balloons be used outdoors?

They can, but heat, wind and rain affect them. Ask which options suit your venue and forecast.
