What Is Included in a Birthday Decoration Package?

A practical 2026 guide to common inclusions, optional add-ons and questions to ask before booking.

By Balloons & Bliss SG · Published 13 July 2026 · 7-minute read

Quick overview

A package may include:

  • Organic balloon garland
  • Personalised backdrop
  • Welcome sign and cake plinths
  • Number display and theme styling
  • Delivery and professional setup
  • Optional teardown

Use the written quotation as the definitive list of inclusions, sizes, quantities and charges.

1. Balloon Garland

Mixed sizes and customised colours create a flowing focal arrangement.

2. Personalised Backdrop

Add a name, message, theme artwork and coordinated colours.

3. Welcome Sign

Choose printed, acrylic, easel or theme-matching signage.

4. Cake Plinths

Elevate the cake with optional props, florals and balloon accents.

5. Number Display

Position a freestanding or illuminated “1” beside the main feature.

6. Theme Styling

Coordinate colours, balloons, artwork, props and table elements.

7. Delivery + Setup

Confirm transport, installation, coverage area and additional charges.

8. Optional Teardown

Confirm availability, timing and cost in the quotation.

Popular add-ons

  • LED number lights
  • Balloon columns or walls
  • Additional cake-table styling
  • Artificial florals and custom cut-outs
  • Neon signs and extra clusters
  • Dessert-table or entrance styling

How to choose a package

Compare venue size, guest count, photography goals and preferred style. A compact event may need one strong backdrop; a larger celebration may add entrance styling or a second photo area.

Questions to ask

  • What exactly is included?
  • Which items are upgrades?
  • Are dimensions and balloon coverage specified?
  • Are delivery and setup included?
  • Can colours and artwork be customised?
  • Is teardown available?
  • Are there access or location charges?

Why professional setup helps

Experienced decorators plan around venue layout, installation, colour coordination, visual balance and setup time, leaving families less to manage before guests arrive.
