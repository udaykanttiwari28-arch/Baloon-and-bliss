The Complete First Birthday Planning Checklist for Parents

A practical 2026 guide to venues, decorations, cakes, food, entertainment and party-day preparation.

By Balloons & Bliss SG · Published 1 July 2026 · 8-minute read

This is a flexible guide. Starting early provides more choices for weekends and popular periods.

1. Decide your budget

List venue rental, decorations, cake, food, photography, entertainment, favours, outfits and miscellaneous costs. Prioritise what matters most to your family.

2. Choose the right venue

Home

Comfortable and budget-friendly for intimate gatherings.

Condo room

Spacious and often suitable for balloon backdrops.

Restaurant

Food and seating in one place with less cleanup.

Hotel or venue

Suitable for larger guest lists and elaborate setups.

3. Choose a birthday theme

Your theme can guide decorations, cake, invitations and outfit. Consider Jungle Safari, Teddy Bear, Dinosaur, Car Racing, Baby Boss, Frozen Inspired, Butterfly Garden, Princess, Unicorn or Floral Garden.

4. Book your decorator

A decorator can help with balloon garlands, personalised backdrops, welcome signs, cake-table styling and professional setup. Share venue photos and measurements when requesting a quote.

5. Order the cake

Consider the theme, servings, allergies, cake topper and whether you want a separate smash cake.

6. Create your guest list

Your guest count affects venue size, catering, seating and decoration requirements. Choose a size that suits your family and baby's comfort.

7. Send invitations

Include date, time, venue, RSVP deadline, dress code and parking or arrival information.

8. Plan food and drinks

Consider buffet catering, restaurant menus, finger foods, desserts and fruit. Account for dietary requirements, children's meals and water.

9. Consider photography

Plan time for family portraits, cake cutting, guest photographs and candid moments. A professional photographer is optional.

10. Plan simple entertainment

Depending on guest ages and space, consider bubbles, face painting, balloon sculpting, magic, soft play or colouring.

11. Prepare your baby's outfit

Choose breathable fabrics and pack spare clothes, wipes and extra diapers.

12. Pack an essentials bag

  • Wipes and diapers
  • Bottles, formula or snacks
  • Extra clothes and towels
  • Required medication
  • Phone charger and safety pins

13. Confirm setup timing

Confirm venue access, loading and lift access, setup duration and completion time with your venue and decorator.

Common mistakes to avoid

Waiting until preferred dates are unavailable, planning too many activities, ignoring nap time or venue restrictions, and forgetting to coordinate backdrop colours with outfits and cake.

Frequently asked questions

When should I start planning?

Starting 8–12 weeks ahead can provide more choices, but a simpler celebration may need less time.

How many guests should I invite?

There is no correct number. Choose a count that fits your venue, budget and preferred atmosphere.

Should I hire a decorator?

A decorator can save time and create a cohesive setup when you want personalised backdrops and balloon styling.

How early should I book decorations?

Book as early as possible after confirming your date and venue.
