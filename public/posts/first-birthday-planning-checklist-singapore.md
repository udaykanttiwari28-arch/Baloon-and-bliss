# The Complete First Birthday Planning Checklist for Parents in Singapore (2026 Guide)

Your baby's first birthday is a meaningful milestone—a celebration of your child's first year and a chance to gather loved ones. Planning can feel overwhelming when you're juggling a venue, guest list, decorations, cake, catering, entertainment and photography while caring for a one-year-old.

This checklist is designed for families in Singapore, whether you're hosting a cosy celebration at home or a larger hotel event.

This is a flexible guide, not a requirement. Starting early gives you more choices for weekends and popular celebration periods.

# Step 1: Decide Your Budget

List likely costs such as venue rental, balloon decorations, cake, food and drinks, photography, entertainment, favours, outfits and miscellaneous items. A realistic budget helps you prioritise what matters most.

# Step 2: Choose the Right Venue

## Home

Comfortable and budget-friendly for small family gatherings. Check available space, furniture arrangement and guest parking.

## Condo Function Room

Often spacious, air-conditioned and suitable for balloon backdrops. Confirm booking rules, access times and cleanup requirements.

## Restaurant

A practical choice when you want food and seating in one place with less cleanup.

## Hotel or Event Venue

Suitable for larger guest lists and more elaborate setups. Ask about loading access, setup windows and decoration restrictions.

# Step 3: Choose a Birthday Theme

The theme can guide your decorations, cake, invitations and baby's outfit. Popular ideas include Jungle Safari, Teddy Bear, Dinosaur, Car Racing, Baby Boss, Frozen Inspired, Butterfly Garden, Princess, Unicorn and Floral Garden.

# Step 4: Book Your Birthday Decorator

A decorator can help with balloon garlands, a personalised backdrop, welcome sign, cake-table styling and professional setup. Share a photo and measurements of the venue when requesting a quote.

# Step 5: Order the Birthday Cake

Consider the theme, number of servings, allergies, cake topper and whether you want a separate smash cake.

# Step 6: Create Your Guest List

Your guest count affects venue size, catering, seating and decoration requirements. Choose the gathering size that suits your family and baby's comfort.

# Step 7: Send Invitations

Include the date, time, venue, RSVP deadline, dress code and parking or arrival information. Digital invitations are convenient and easy to update.

# Step 8: Plan Food and Drinks

Options include buffet catering, restaurant menus, finger foods, desserts and fruit. Account for dietary requirements, children's meals and drinking water.

# Step 9: Consider Photography

A professional photographer is optional. Plan time for family portraits, cake cutting, guest photographs and candid moments.

# Step 10: Plan Simple Entertainment

Depending on guest ages and venue space, consider bubbles, face painting, balloon sculpting, magic, soft play or colouring. A relaxed schedule may work better for smaller celebrations.

# Step 11: Prepare Your Baby's Outfit

Choose breathable fabric and comfortable footwear. Pack spare clothes, wipes and extra diapers.

# Step 12: Pack a Party-Day Essentials Bag

- Baby wipes and diapers
- Bottles, formula or snacks
- Extra clothes and towels
- Required medication
- Phone charger
- Safety pins

# Step 13: Confirm Setup Timing

Confirm venue access, loading and lift access, setup duration and the required completion time with your venue and decorator.

# Common Planning Mistakes

- Waiting until preferred vendors or dates are unavailable
- Planning too many activities for a one-year-old
- Ignoring the baby's usual nap time
- Overlooking venue restrictions
- Forgetting to coordinate backdrop colours with outfits and cake

# Frequently Asked Questions

## When should I start planning?

Starting 8–12 weeks ahead can provide more venue and vendor choices, but a simpler celebration may need less time.

## How many guests should I invite?

There is no correct number. Choose a guest count that fits your venue, budget and the atmosphere you want.

## Should I hire a professional decorator?

A decorator can save time and create a cohesive setup when you want personalised backdrops and balloon styling.

## How early should I book decorations?

Book as early as possible after confirming your date and venue, especially for weekends and popular periods.

# Final Thoughts

A first birthday is about celebrating an important milestone, not achieving perfection. Start early, focus on what matters to your family and keep the day comfortable for your baby.
