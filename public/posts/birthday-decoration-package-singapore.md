# What Is Included in a Birthday Decoration Package in Singapore? (2026 Guide)

Every decorator and package is different, but birthday decoration services may combine balloon styling, personalised backdrops, setup and decorative elements to create a celebration space.

Understanding the exact inclusions helps you compare quotations and choose an option that suits your venue, style and budget.

# Quick Overview

A birthday decoration package may include:

- Organic balloon garland
- Personalised backdrop
- Welcome sign
- Cake plinths
- Number display
- Theme styling
- Delivery and on-site setup
- Optional teardown

Always use the written quotation as the definitive list of inclusions, sizes, quantities and charges.

# 1. Organic Balloon Garland

Organic garlands use several balloon sizes for a flowing appearance. Colours and coverage can be adapted to the theme, venue, backdrop and photography area.

# 2. Personalised Birthday Backdrop

A backdrop can include the baby's name, birthday message, theme artwork and coordinated colours, creating a dedicated setting for family portraits.

# 3. Welcome Sign

Options may include printed foam board, acrylic signage, easel displays or a theme-matching board personalised with the celebrant's name.

# 4. Cake Plinths and Display Area

Plinths elevate the cake and may be styled with props, balloon accents, artificial florals or theme accessories.

# 5. Number Display

A freestanding or illuminated number “1” can sit beside the backdrop, cake display or entrance.

# 6. Theme Styling

Coordinated colours, balloon arrangements, backdrop artwork, props, signage and table elements create a consistent visual direction.

# 7. Delivery and Professional Setup

Depending on the quotation, service can cover transport, installation, positioning and final styling. Confirm the delivery area, access requirements and any extra charges.

# 8. Optional Teardown

Some quotations may offer post-event balloon removal, backdrop dismantling and packing of reusable items. Confirm availability, timing and cost before booking.

# Popular Add-Ons

- LED number lights
- Balloon columns or walls
- Additional cake-table styling
- Artificial florals
- Custom name cut-outs
- Neon signs
- Extra balloon clusters
- Dessert-table or entrance styling

# How to Choose the Right Package

## Venue Size

Large rooms may need wider or taller features for visual impact, while compact venues benefit from focused arrangements.

## Guest Count

An intimate event may need one backdrop; larger celebrations may add an entrance or second photo area.

## Photography Goals

Prioritise the backdrop and cake area if family portraits and cake-cutting photographs matter most.

## Celebration Style

Choose a package that supports your preferred minimalist, elegant or colourful themed look.

# Questions to Ask Before Booking

- What exactly is included?
- Which items are upgrades?
- Are sizes and balloon coverage specified?
- Are delivery and setup included?
- Can colours and artwork be customised?
- Is teardown available?
- Are there access or location charges?
